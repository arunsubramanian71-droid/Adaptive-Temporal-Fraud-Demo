## Addendum, 2026-10-22 (the text above is the trace as written on 2026-10-21)

* **P13-7, real browser:** `scripts/verify_dashboard_browser.py` (headless Chrome 156 through Playwright) checked the page at desktop 1366x900 and phone 390x844 in light and dark colour schemes: 123 of 123 checks after fixes, record and 25 hashed screenshots in `test_results/browser/`. The first run on the 2026-10-21 page failed 13 checks: dark-scheme button contrast 2.51:1, a double click sending three requests and drawing two tables, a banner stuck on "Loading" after a failed request, no tab-panel semantics, arrow keys, live regions or explicit focus style, and clipped or squeezed content at phone width. All were fixed in `src/serving/static/dashboard.html` (`submission/FINAL_COMPLETION_RECORD.md` section 6C).
* **P13-2 / P13-3, input errors:** probing the running server found 500 responses for a text or boolean `TransactionID` and for a deeply nested body, and a fractional id reported back rounded. They are now 400 responses with explanations (`tests/test_serving.py`, `tests/test_inference_demo.py`, `tests/test_research_serving.py`).
* **P13-8, mutation check:** the recorded "12 of 14" was stale. Since the research mode was added, mutant M06's pattern occurred twice and the mutant was silently not applied (a re-run would have exited 1). M06 is now anchored, and two mutants guard this pass's fixes: 14 of 16 detected, 2 equivalent (`test_results/test_sensitivity.json`). One unexplained event: in one of the five runs of the mutation check on 2026-10-22, mutant M05 (registered as equivalent) was caught by `test_p13_2_status_codes_and_json_errors`; the exception was not recorded at the time. 120 targeted repetitions of that test (60 on the real code, 60 with M05) all passed, and the other four runs were unchanged, so the cause (most likely a transient local network error) is not established. The script now records the exception of every failing test and flags an equivalent mutant that is caught.
* **What the browser check still does not establish:** behaviour in browsers other than Chrome (Edge is supported by the script but was not run), a screen-reader session, load, or anything about the original Phase 13 scope.
